Privacy Policy for Spender Pro

Spender Pro is designed from the ground up to respect your financial privacy. All card tracking, budget metrics, and payment notification parsing take place strictly within your local Android device sandbox.

Effective Date: September 9, 2026
Package ID: com.budget.planning
100% Offline
No internet connection required. No background cloud sync or API uploads.
AES-256 Encrypted
Local financial databases are securely encrypted using standard hardware keys.
Zero Trackers
No Firebase Analytics, Google Ads, or third-party telemetry SDKs included.
1

Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Spender Pro ("the App") is committed to protecting your personal and financial privacy. The App is built on a strict 100% on-device, offline architecture. All financial tracking, card carousel limits, budget computations, and notification scanning occur exclusively inside your local Android sandbox.

Zero Server Transmission: No personal, financial, card details, or transaction data are ever collected, transmitted to remote servers, sold, or shared with third parties under any circumstances.
2

Information We Access and How It Is Used

A. Bank Payment Notifications & SMS Header Scanning

  • Access: Spender Pro accesses status bar bank notifications and official financial sender titles (such as TRAI headers including HDFCBK, SBICRD, ICICIB).
  • Purpose: To automatically parse transaction amounts, log credit/debit card expenses in real time, and compute current card balances without manual entry.
  • Local Processing: All parsing algorithms operate 100% locally on your smartphone processor. Raw notification contents are never uploaded to external servers or cloud databases.

B. Financial & Budget Configuration Data

  • Access: User-configured card limits, warning boundaries, card names, custom category labels, and daily transaction logs.
  • Purpose: To render real-time card carousel metrics, analytics charts, and trigger system alerts when spending approaches preset thresholds.
  • Encryption: All stored expense data and card settings are encrypted locally using AES-256 encryption before being saved to Android private local app storage.

C. System Permissions Matrix

Permission Identifier Purpose in App Storage Location
BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE Required to listen to incoming status bar bank notifications for automatic expense calculation. Device RAM (Volatile local parsing)
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required to dispatch local push notifications when card limits reach warning or maximum boundaries. Local System Queue
3

Data Storage and Retention

All application data is retained solely within the private application directory on your physical device.

  • Local Sandbox: No external backup databases, cloud servers, or remote sync tools are utilized.
  • User Control & Instant Deletion: You retain complete ownership of your data. You can erase all data at any time by uninstalling Spender Pro or clicking Clear App Storage / Cache in Android Settings. Once cleared, your data is permanently destroyed as no cloud recovery backups exist.
4

Third-Party Services and Analytics

Spender Pro contains zero third-party SDKs, tracking pixels, advertising networks, or remote telemetry frameworks (such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or AdMob).

Your app usage patterns, device diagnostic data, and financial activity are never monitored or transmitted to third parties.

5

Children's Privacy

Spender Pro is designed for general adult audiences seeking personal finance management tools. The App does not knowingly request or collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

6

AES-256 Security Measures

We prioritize the security of your financial data. Because all calculations take place locally and database files are protected with industry-standard AES-256 encryption within the Android OS app sandbox, your information remains safe from unauthorized remote interception or extraction.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory standards. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
